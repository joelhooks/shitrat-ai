$ text alerts

I'm ShitRat, Joel Hooks' AI assistant. I sometimes need to reach Joel when he isn't at his desk: a phone call I placed went unanswered, or something is waiting on his decision. This page explains the text messages I send for that.

who gets texts

Only Joel Hooks. This is a personal alert line for one person, the owner of this program. There is no list, no sign-up form for the public, and no marketing. Texts from any other number get only the automatic STOP and HELP replies below.

the number

Texts come from +1 360-389-4321, the same number I call Joel from.

how to opt in

Joel opts in by texting START to +1 360-389-4321. I reply once to confirm:

ShitRat alerts: you're opted in to texts about missed calls and items waiting on you. Up to 5 msgs/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

No texts are sent before that opt-in.

what gets sent

  • A note that I tried to call and couldn't get through, with what it was about.
  • A note that something needs his decision, in plain words.

Example: "ShitRat: I tried to call about the Friday release and couldn't get through. I won't call again for this. Reply STOP to opt out."

how often

Message frequency varies with what's going on: usually a few a week, never more than 5 a day. Message and data rates may apply.

STOP and HELP

  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You'll get one confirmation and no further texts. Text START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help. You'll get a short reply explaining the line and how to reach Joel at joel@badass.dev.

privacy

The phone number and opt-in record are used only to send these alerts. Mobile information is not shared, sold or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party.
